Support

A little help, when you need it.

Any Day Now is being prepared for its first App Store release. This page also supports people using a development preview. Exact labels may change as the app is finished.

Ask a question or report a problem

Open a support issue on GitHub. A GitHub account is needed to post. Include the app version, iPhone or Watch model, system version, what you expected and what happened.

Support issues are public. Do not include pregnancy records, due dates, baby names, health details, exports, backups, receipts or screenshots containing private information. The developer has no copy of your records to recover.

Your timers and your care team

A movement target is a recording preference, not a health check. A contraction timer measures the times you enter; it does not predict labor or say when to travel. Your midwife or doctor's instructions come first. If you have a concern about movements or your pregnancy, contact your care team rather than waiting for an app target or timer.

Pro and restoring a purchase

Pro is one optional App Store purchase, not a subscription. In the app, open Settings, then Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account used for the purchase, or an eligible Family Sharing account. A development preview may use Apple's testing environment; a preview entitlement is not proof of an App Store purchase.

Family Sharing shares Pro access. It does not share pregnancy records or a live timer between different Apple Accounts. Partner record sharing is not part of the current version.

Export and backups

In Settings, under Data, use Export all records as CSV. Then choose Share CSV to save or send the file. CSV export stays free.

Use Create backup, then Share backup to save a JSON copy somewhere you control. Restore a backup imports a saved copy and merges its records with those already on the device; newer changes are kept. Restoring is not a way to erase the records already present. A local backup is not a guarantee of recovery after device loss or app removal; save a separate export somewhere you control if you need an independent copy. Keep exports and backups private.

The app also writes dated local backups when it starts. A backup may contain older entries that no longer appear in your normal history. These copies are stored with the app's documents; a manual export gives you a copy outside the app.

Phone and paired Watch

The 1.0 release scope keeps health records on the iPhone and its paired Watch and exchanges copies directly through Apple's Watch Connectivity. It does not automatically store health records in iCloud or share records between Apple Accounts. Watch delivery depends on pairing, connection and system services; it is not a second-phone synchronization service.

The release candidate is being changed to this local-record scope. Earlier development previews may have used private CloudKit storage. Keep an export before removing a preview or changing devices; this change does not promise to erase older copies or restore lost records. Only harmless preferences may synchronize through Apple's key-value service.

Removing records

Deleting an entry from history is different from removing every copy. Read Removing your records before deleting the app, discarding backups or removing data from an earlier development preview.
