Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Your records stay with you.

Any Day Now stores pregnancy and newborn records on your iPhone and paired Watch, with direct exchange through Apple’s Watch Connectivity. It does not automatically store those records in iCloud. The developer does not run a server for these records and does not receive them. There are no ads, tracking, analytics SDKs, developer accounts or AI features.

The app is being prepared for its first App Store release.

Records on your devices

The information you enter can include due dates and their calculation dates, birth dates, an optional baby name, movements, contraction times, feeding, diapers, sleep, pumping, notes and checklists. The app stores these records in its local database. Its widgets and related extensions use the app's shared storage to read and update records. Harmless preferences such as language, appearance, units, haptics and keeping the screen awake may synchronize through Apple’s key-value service. Health records, baby names, due dates, count targets and Pro entitlement are not sent through that service.

Widgets and Live Activities can show counts, timing and other selected activity information on the Lock Screen. Consider who can see your screen. You control widgets and Lock Screen access through Apple's system settings.

Paired Watch

The iPhone app and its paired Watch app can exchange activity state and copies of records through Apple's Watch Connectivity. Transfer files can temporarily remain in app storage while delivery is pending; the app removes its outgoing transfer file after the transfer completes. Records stored by the Watch app remain on that device until its app data is removed.

Backups and exports

The app writes a dated JSON backup when it starts and normally keeps up to 14 dated local backups. Backups may include records marked as deleted. They are stored locally with the app. These files are not a separate off-device recovery service. The app also creates temporary files when you export CSV, create a PDF report or share a manual backup.

When you save or share an export or backup, that copy goes to the location or recipient you choose. The developer does not receive a copy. Files you save elsewhere are not deleted when you remove the app. The 1.0 release excludes its health-record directories from operating-system backups. A user-chosen export can still be saved to a cloud location, including iCloud Drive, through the system share sheet. You control that choice. Keep a separate export if you need a copy outside the app; automatic restoration is not guaranteed.

Removing your records

In-app deletion hides an entry from normal history and stores a deletion marker for synchronization and merging. It does not promise immediate erasure of the database contents, backups or exported copies.

To remove copies under your control, first keep any export you want to retain. Stop using and delete the app and its data from every iPhone and Watch where it is installed; offloading an app may retain its data. Delete any backups, exports or shared copies you saved elsewhere. Manage older device/computer backups separately. A paired device that still retains records can exchange them again.

The developer has no copy of your records to retrieve or erase on your behalf.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple processes Pro purchases and restores. The app checks signed purchase entitlements to decide whether Pro is available. The developer does not receive your payment details. Apple provides sales and financial reports through its developer services. Family Sharing shares eligible Pro access; it does not share pregnancy records.

Optional reminders use local notifications. Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and Live Activities use Apple's platform services for their functions. The app does not use HealthKit.

Diagnostics

The app does not add health records to its diagnostic logging or send them to an analytics service. If you choose to share diagnostics with developers in your device settings, Apple may provide crash or diagnostic reports under its own policies. This policy does not claim that Apple collects no platform information.

This website and support

This static website uses no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or external fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose processing of website requests is covered by the GitHub privacy statement. Support issues on GitHub are public. Do not post health information or private files there.

For questions, visit Support.
